# 💡 IDEAS FOR 🩺 StudyMed 🧠

## 👆 Overview

_**🧠MedStudy**_ is a prospective telegram medical study bot and in the future a cross-platform software (standalone app, web and telegram based bot), that aims to assist medical students in their day to day studies. Its core dependency is the **aiogram** library.

## 🫀 Core Features

- **🧫** Generation of questions and their answers from user created _**Knowledge cards**_.
- **🧩** Quiz sessions to test the user's 🧠 knowledge in a subject.
- **📊 Statistics and score tracking;** quiz sessions based on the user's performance during quizzes, to effectively curate more weak-areas focused quizzes.
- 🧓 Predefined questions in database
- 🛃 Custom inline keyboard for **UI** & **UX** 😊 friendliness.
- 🚮 Delete flashcards or regenerate multiple questions and answers from already existing flashcards
- 🔔 Send study reminders to user's based on the set study time

## 🔮 Later Features

- 🧩👨‍👩‍👦 Community shared resources (knowledge cards / question & answers).
- 🤖 AI validation of created knowledge cards (to prevent inaccurate medical knowledge).
- Community 💯 **scoreboards**, for healthy competitions.
- **🤖 Bot managed group;** for connecting with other **MedStudy** users.
- Standalone app, with offline support.
- Migration to PostgreSQL or SQLAlchemy for database.
